# Monnai

> Monnai is a global consumer-insights infrastructure company that delivers real-time, AI-ready data for decisioning across the customer lifecycle. Its Insights API unifies fragmented identity, device, and behavioral signals into clean, contextual profiles that power acquisition, onboarding, risk assessment, credit decisioning, and collections for financial institutions, fintechs, ecommerce, and marketplaces across 190+ countries.
